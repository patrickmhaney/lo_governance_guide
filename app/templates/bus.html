The bus matrix shows what each process can be analyzed by. Business processes run down the side. Dimensions run across the top: the things the business slices numbers by, like customer, product, date or site.

  1. Tick a box when you want to see that process by that dimension.
  2. A dimension ticked for several processes is shared. It's defined once, so every report that uses it agrees.
  3. Dashed boxes are suggestions from the breakdowns on your measures. Click one to tick it.
  4. Pick a dashboard to see which processes its measures come from, and which filters work across all of it.
